Whether you're a solo creator or part of a growing team, Gugu gives you the tools to organize your work, collaborate with others, and access everything programmatically through a clean API.

## Get started in three steps

New to Gugu? Follow this quick onboarding flow to go from zero to productive in just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for a free account">
    Head to [app.gugu.app](https://app.gugu.app) and create your account using your email address or an existing Google or GitHub account. No credit card is required to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and explore your dashboard">
    Once your account is created, log in to access your personal dashboard. From here you can create content, manage your workspace, and invite collaborators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using Gugu">
    Create your first piece of content, apply tags, organize it into a collection, and use search to find it instantly. You're ready to go.
  </Step>
</Steps>
