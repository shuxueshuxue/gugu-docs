This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a brand-new account to actively using Gugu's core features. By the end, you'll have a workspace set up, a piece of content created and tagged, a successful search under your belt, and your first API call made. No prior experience with Gugu is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Navigate to [app.gugu.app](https://app.gugu.app) and click **Sign Up**. You can register with your email address or use one-click sign-up via Google or GitHub.

    Fill in your name, email, and a strong password, then click **Create Account**. Gugu will send you a verification email — open it and click the confirmation link to activate your account.

    <Note>
      If you don't see the verification email within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder. You can also request a new one from the login page.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and access your dashboard">
    Once your email is verified, return to [app.gugu.app](https://app.gugu.app) and log in with your credentials. You'll land directly on your **Dashboard**.

    Your dashboard is your home base in Gugu. From here you can:

    - View recent content and activity
    - Navigate to your collections and tags
    - Manage your workspace settings
    - Invite collaborators to your team

    Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the sidebar navigation — it gives you quick access to every major section of the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first piece of content">
    In the sidebar, click **Content**, then click the **New Content** button in the top-right corner of the page.

    Give your content a title, add a body, and apply one or more tags to help organize it. When you're happy with it, click **Save**. Your content is now stored in your workspace and immediately available in search.

    <Tip>
      Use descriptive tags from the start — for example, `project-alpha` or `meeting-notes` — so your workspace stays organized as it grows.
    </Tip>

    To add your content to a collection, open the content item, click the **Collections** panel on the right, and select an existing collection or create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use search to find your content">
    Click the **Search** icon in the top navigation bar or press `Cmd+K` (Mac) / `Ctrl+K` (Windows and Linux) to open the search panel.

    Type a word or phrase from the content you just created. Gugu searches titles, body text, tags, and metadata in real time, so your result should appear immediately.

    You can refine results using filters for content type, date range, tags, and collections. Click any result to open it directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make your first API call

Once you're comfortable with the Gugu interface, you can start using the API to interact with your content programmatically. The example below retrieves a list of all content items in your workspace.

<Tip>
  You can find your API key in **Settings → API Keys**. Click **Generate New Key**, give it a name, and copy the key value — you'll use it as the `Authorization` header in every API request.
</Tip>

```bash
curl --request GET \
  --url https://api.gugu.app/v1/content \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json"
```

A successful response returns a JSON array of your content items:

```json
{
  "data": [
    {
      "id": "cnt_01hx9z3kqr8f2p",
      "title": "My First Content Item",
      "tags": ["getting-started", "example"],
      "created_at": "2024-06-01T10:23:00Z",
      "updated_at": "2024-06-01T10:23:00Z"
    }
  ],
  "meta": {
    "total": 1,
    "page": 1,
    "per_page": 20
  }
}
```

Replace `YOUR_API_KEY` with the actual key you copied from Settings. You can use any HTTP client — `curl`, Postman, Insomnia, or your preferred programming language's HTTP library.

<Note>
  Keep your API key private. Do not commit it to source control or share it publicly. If a key is ever compromised, you can revoke it from **Settings → API Keys** and generate a new one immediately.
</Note>
