The Gugu dashboard is the first thing you see when you sign in, and it's designed to give you immediate orientation across your entire workspace. From here you can check what's changed, jump into your content library, kick off common tasks, and track how your workspace is being used — without having to hunt through multiple menus.

## Dashboard Sections

The dashboard is divided into four main sections, each giving you a focused view of a different aspect of your workspace.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Recent Activity" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    See a chronological feed of the latest changes across your workspace — edits, new uploads, comments, and collaborator actions. Click any activity item to jump directly to the affected content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content Library" icon="folder-open">
    Browse and manage every piece of content in your workspace from one place. Filter by type, tag, or date, and open any item for a full-screen edit or preview.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quick Actions" icon="bolt">
    One-click shortcuts for the tasks you do most often: creating a new document, uploading a file, inviting a teammate, or starting a search. Customize which shortcuts appear here from **Settings → Dashboard**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    Visualize usage trends across your workspace — content views, search queries, active users, and storage consumption. Use the date picker to compare different time periods.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Pinning Items

You can pin frequently accessed content directly to your dashboard for one-click access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the item you want to pin">
    Locate it in the **Content Library** section or via search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the context menu">
    Hover over the item and click the **⋯** (more options) icon that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Pin to Dashboard">
    Click **Pin to Dashboard**. The item appears in a **Pinned** row at the top of your dashboard immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To unpin an item, hover over it in the Pinned row, click **⋯**, and select **Unpin**.

## Using the Sidebar

The sidebar on the left side of the screen gives you persistent navigation to every major section of Gugu.

- **Collapse or expand** the sidebar by clicking the **‹** / **›** arrow at its top edge, or pressing <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd>.
- **Reorder sections** by dragging them up or down in the sidebar — your order is saved per device.
- **Favorites** — star any page or folder in the sidebar to promote it to a dedicated Favorites section at the top.

## Switching Between Grid and List View

The **Content Library** section and most other content-browsing panels let you toggle between two display modes.

| View | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Grid** | Scanning visual thumbnails of images, documents, and media |
| **List** | Comparing metadata (dates, tags, authors) across many items at once |

Click the **grid** (⊞) or **list** (☰) icon in the top-right corner of any content panel to switch. Gugu remembers your preference separately for each section.

<Tip>
  Gugu has a full set of keyboard shortcuts to keep your hands on the keyboard. Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere in the dashboard to open the **Keyboard Shortcuts** reference panel. Some highlights:

  | Shortcut | Action |
  |---|---|
  | <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> | Open search |
  | <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> | Create new document |
  | <kbd>Cmd/Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> | Toggle sidebar |
  | <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>D</kbd> | Go to Dashboard |
  | <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>L</kbd> | Go to Content Library |
</Tip>
