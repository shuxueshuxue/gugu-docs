Settings is where you make Gugu your own. From updating your profile photo to configuring two-factor authentication, generating API keys, and managing your subscription, everything that controls how Gugu looks and behaves for you lives here. Changes you make in Settings take effect immediately unless otherwise noted.

## Accessing Settings

You can open Settings in two ways:

- Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner of any page and choose **Settings** from the dropdown menu.
- Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd> on macOS or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd> on Windows and Linux from anywhere in the app.

## Settings Sections

<Accordion title="Profile">
  Your profile controls how you appear to teammates and collaborators across your workspace.

  | Field | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **Display name** | The name shown next to your activity, comments, and shared content. |
  | **Avatar** | Upload a photo or choose a generated avatar. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP (max 5 MB). |
  | **Bio** | A short description of yourself visible on your public profile page. |
  | **Email address** | The address used for sign-in and notifications. Changing it triggers a confirmation email to your new address. |

  Click **Save changes** after editing any field.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Security">
  Keep your account secure by managing your password, enabling two-factor authentication, and reviewing active sessions.

  **Password**

  Click **Change password**, enter your current password, then set and confirm a new one. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.

  **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

  Enable 2FA to require a one-time code from an authenticator app on every sign-in. Click **Enable** and follow the on-screen steps to scan a QR code with your authenticator app. See the [Authentication guide](/features/authentication) for a full walkthrough.

  **Active Sessions**

  The **Active Sessions** list shows every device and browser currently signed into your account, including location and last-seen time. Click **Revoke** next to any session you don't recognize to sign it out immediately. Use **Revoke all other sessions** to sign out everywhere except your current device.

  <Warning>
    If you see an active session you don't recognize, revoke it immediately and change your password. Consider enabling 2FA if you haven't already.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="API Keys">
  API keys allow external tools, scripts, and integrations to authenticate with Gugu's REST API using your account permissions.

  **Generating a key**

  1. Click **Generate New Key**.
  2. Give the key a descriptive name (e.g., `GitHub Actions`, `Zapier`).
  3. Optionally set an expiration date.
  4. Click **Create** — your key is shown **once**, so copy it immediately and store it somewhere safe.

  **Viewing your keys**

  The API Keys table shows each key's name, creation date, expiration date (if set), and the last time it was used. Full key values are never shown again after creation; only the last four characters are visible for identification.

  **Revoking a key**

  Click **Revoke** next to any key to invalidate it immediately. This cannot be undone — any integration using that key will stop working until you provide a new one.

  <Note>
    For full instructions on using API keys in requests, see the [Authentication guide](/features/authentication).
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notifications">
  Control exactly when and how Gugu notifies you so your inbox and notification center stay relevant.

  **Email notifications**

  Toggle individual email notification types on or off:

  - **Mentions** — someone @-mentions you in a comment or document.
  - **Comments** — new comments on content you own or follow.
  - **Sharing** — someone shares a file or folder with you.
  - **Workspace digest** — a weekly summary of activity across your workspace.
  - **Security alerts** — sign-ins from new devices or locations.

  **In-app notifications**

  In-app notifications appear in the bell (🔔) menu in the top navigation bar. You can enable or disable each notification type independently from email, giving you the flexibility to get real-time in-app alerts without filling your inbox.

  Click **Save preferences** after making changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Appearance">
  Adjust how Gugu looks to suit your environment and personal taste.

  **Theme**

  | Option | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **Light** | White backgrounds and dark text — ideal for bright environments. |
  | **Dark** | Dark backgrounds and light text — easier on the eyes in low-light settings. |
  | **System** | Automatically follows your operating system's light/dark mode setting. |

  **Display density**

  | Option | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **Comfortable** | Generous spacing between items — the default. |
  | **Compact** | Tighter spacing to fit more content on screen at once. |

  Theme and density changes apply instantly — no page reload required.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Billing">
  Manage your Gugu subscription, monitor your usage, and access past invoices.

  **Current plan**

  Your active plan is displayed at the top of the Billing page, along with your next renewal date and the payment method on file. Click **Change plan** to upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and annual billing.

  **Usage**

  The usage panel shows how much of your plan's allowances you've consumed for the current billing period — storage, API requests, seats, and any other metered features. A progress bar turns amber at 80 % and red at 95 % to give you early warning before you hit a limit.

  **Invoices**

  All past invoices are listed in a table with the date, amount, and status. Click **Download** on any row to save a PDF copy. Invoices are also emailed automatically to your billing contact address after each successful payment.

  <Info>
    If you need to update your billing email, VAT number, or company name on invoices, click **Edit billing details** at the top of the Billing page.
  </Info>
</Accordion>
