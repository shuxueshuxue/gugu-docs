Gugu gives you a flexible set of tools to keep your content structured and easy to find as your workspace grows. Whether you're working with a handful of documents or hundreds of items, you can use tags and collections to organize everything, bulk operations to apply changes at scale, and archiving to keep your active view clean without losing anything permanently.

## Creating a new content item

Use the steps below whenever you want to add something new to your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New item dialog">
    Click the **New item** button in the top-right corner of the dashboard, or press <kbd>N</kbd> from anywhere in the app. A dialog appears asking you to choose a content type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a content type">
    Select the type that best fits what you're creating — for example, **Article**, **Note**, or **Page**. The editor that loads next is tailored to the type you pick.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write and format your content">
    Give the item a title, then start writing in the editor. Use the toolbar or Markdown shortcuts to format text, add images, embed links, and insert code blocks. All changes save automatically as you type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add tags and assign a collection">
    In the right-hand panel, add one or more tags to describe the item's topic or status. Then select a **Collection** from the dropdown to file the item in the right place. Both fields are optional but recommended for keeping things organized.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish the item">
    Click **Publish** to make the item visible to other workspace members with the appropriate permissions. If you're not ready to share it yet, click **Save as draft** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing existing content

To edit any item, click on its title from the dashboard to open it, then click the **Edit** button in the top-right corner — or press <kbd>E</kbd> while the item is focused in the list. The editor opens with all existing content intact. Make your changes and click **Save** (or use <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>). Gugu maintains a full version history, so you can roll back to any previous version from the **History** tab.

## Tagging content

Tags let you apply lightweight, cross-collection labels to your content. You can add tags while creating an item (see step 4 above) or at any time by opening an item and editing the **Tags** field in the right-hand panel. Use consistent, short tags like `draft`, `review`, `published`, or topic-specific labels like `onboarding` or `api-docs` to make filtering fast and reliable.

## Organizing content into collections

Collections work like folders — they group related items together and appear in the left sidebar for quick navigation. To create a new collection, click the **+** icon next to **Collections** in the sidebar and give it a name. Drag items into collections from the dashboard, or assign a collection from inside the item editor. Collections can be nested up to three levels deep, giving you room to build a hierarchy that mirrors your project or team structure.

## Bulk-selecting items and applying tags

When you need to update several items at once, bulk selection saves you a lot of time.

1. Hover over any item in the dashboard list to reveal the checkbox on the left.
2. Check the boxes for all the items you want to update. You can also click the top checkbox to select everything on the current page.
3. A floating action bar appears at the bottom of the screen showing the number of selected items.
4. Click **Add tags**, choose the tags you want to apply, and click **Confirm**. The tags are added to all selected items instantly.

You can also use the bulk action bar to move items to a collection, change their status, or export them in one go.

## Archiving and deleting content

**Archiving** an item removes it from your main dashboard view without permanently deleting it. Archived items remain searchable and can be restored at any time. To archive an item, hover over it in the list, click the **⋯** menu, and select **Archive**. To view archived items, click **Archived** in the left sidebar filters.

**Deleting** an item is permanent and cannot be undone after the 30-day grace period. To delete an item, open its **⋯** menu and select **Delete**. You'll see a confirmation prompt before anything is removed. Deleted items sit in the **Trash** for 30 days, during which you can restore them. After 30 days they are purged automatically.

<Warning>
  Deleting an item also removes all comments and version history attached to it. Archive instead of delete whenever you're unsure — you can always clean up the archive later.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Keyboard shortcuts speed up your content workflow significantly. Press <kbd>N</kbd> to create a new item from anywhere, <kbd>E</kbd> to jump straight into editing a focused item, and <kbd>/</kbd> to open the command palette for quick actions like tagging, moving, or archiving.
</Tip>
