Gugu is built for teams. Every workspace supports real-time co-editing, fine-grained permission roles, shareable links, and threaded comments so that your whole team can work on content together without stepping on each other's toes. This guide explains how to set up your team and get the most out of Gugu's collaboration features.

## Inviting team members

You can invite as many collaborators as your plan allows directly from workspace settings.

1. Click your workspace avatar in the top-left corner and select **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Workspace → Members**.
3. Click the **Invite member** button.
4. Enter the person's email address, select a permission role (see the table below), and click **Send invite**.

The invitee receives an email with a secure link. Once they accept, they appear in your **Members** list and can access the workspace according to their assigned role.

<Note>
  Pending invites appear in the **Members** list with a **Pending** badge. You can resend or revoke an invite at any time by clicking the **⋯** menu next to the invite.
</Note>

## Permission levels

Gugu offers three roles that let you control exactly what each person can do inside your workspace.

| Role | View | Edit | Admin |
|------|:----:|:----:|:-----:|
| Viewer | ✓ | — | — |
| Editor | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Admin | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

- **Viewer** — can read all published content in the workspace but cannot make any changes.
- **Editor** — can create, edit, and delete content items they have access to, but cannot manage members or workspace settings.
- **Admin** — has full access including managing members, changing roles, editing workspace settings, and managing integrations.

You can change a member's role at any time from **Settings → Workspace → Members** by clicking the role badge next to their name.

## Real-time collaboration and conflict resolution

When two or more people open the same content item simultaneously, Gugu automatically activates live co-editing mode. You'll see each collaborator's cursor highlighted in a distinct color alongside their avatar. Every keystroke syncs in real time — there's no need to manually save or refresh.

If a network interruption causes two edits to conflict, Gugu uses operational transformation to merge changes automatically. In the rare case where automatic merging isn't possible, the app shows a **Conflict resolution** dialog that presents both versions side by side and lets you choose which text to keep, or manually combine them.

<Info>
  Real-time collaboration requires both editors to be online. If you work offline, your changes are queued locally and synced the next time you reconnect. Gugu will notify you if any queued changes conflict with edits made by others while you were offline.
</Info>

## Sharing a content item via link

You can share any published content item with people outside your workspace using a shareable link — no Gugu account required.

1. Open the content item you want to share.
2. Click the **Share** button in the top-right corner of the editor.
3. Toggle **Enable public link** to the on position.
4. Copy the generated link and send it to whoever needs access.

Public links give read-only access to the published version of the item. You can disable the link at any time by toggling **Enable public link** off — the link becomes invalid immediately.

<Warning>
  Anyone with a public link can view the item, even if they're not a workspace member. Avoid sharing links to items that contain sensitive or confidential information.
</Warning>

## Comments and @mentions

Threaded comments let your team discuss specific parts of a content item without cluttering the document itself.

**Leaving a comment:**
Highlight any text in the editor, then click the speech-bubble icon that appears in the margin, or right-click and choose **Add comment**. Type your comment in the panel that slides out on the right, then press **Enter** to post it. A colored annotation marker appears over the highlighted text so everyone can see exactly what the comment refers to.

**Replying to a comment:**
Click on any existing comment in the comments panel to expand the thread, type your reply, and press **Enter**.

**Using @mentions:**
Type `@` followed by a team member's name inside any comment to notify them directly. They receive an in-app notification and an email summary letting them know they were mentioned and linking them straight to the comment.

**Resolving comments:**
Once a discussion is closed, click the **Resolve** button on the comment thread. Resolved comments are hidden from the default view but remain accessible under **Show resolved** in the comments panel for future reference.

<Tip>
  Use @mentions when you need a specific person to take action on a piece of content — for example, `@alex can you review the second paragraph?`. This creates a clear, trackable thread of responsibility without needing a separate message in chat.
</Tip>
