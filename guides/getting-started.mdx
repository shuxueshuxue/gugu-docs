This guide walks you through everything you need to hit the ground running with Gugu — from signing up for your account all the way to creating your first piece of content and bringing a collaborator on board. By the end, your workspace will be ready for real work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Gugu account">
    Head to [https://app.gugu.app/signup](https://app.gugu.app/signup) and enter your name, email address, and a strong password. You can also sign up with a Google or GitHub account for a faster start. Once you submit the form, Gugu sends a verification email to the address you provided.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Open the email from Gugu and click the **Verify my email** button. This confirms your address and activates your account. If you don't see the email within a couple of minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend verification email** on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your workspace">
    After verifying your email you land on the workspace setup screen. Give your workspace a clear, recognizable name — your team will see this name throughout the app. Upload an avatar (a logo works great here) to make your workspace easy to identify at a glance. Click **Save and continue** when you're happy with the details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first content item">
    From the dashboard, click the **New item** button in the top-right corner (or press <kbd>N</kbd>). Choose a content type, give the item a title, and start writing in the editor. When you're ready, click **Publish** to make it live inside your workspace. Your first content item is now saved and ready to be organized, shared, or built upon.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite a collaborator (optional)">
    Want to bring someone else in right away? Navigate to **Settings → Workspace → Members** and click **Invite member**. Enter their email address, choose a permission role, and click **Send invite**. They'll receive an email with a link to join your workspace. You can skip this step for now and invite people later at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you're a developer looking to access Gugu programmatically, check out the [Quickstart guide](/quickstart) for API keys, authentication, and your first API request.
</Note>
