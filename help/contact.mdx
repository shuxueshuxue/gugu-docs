## How to write an effective support request

A well-structured support request helps us understand your issue immediately and get you to a resolution faster. When you contact us, please include the following information.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe what you were trying to do">
    Explain the action you were taking — for example, "I was trying to invite a team member from the Settings page." Include the feature area and any relevant content or item names.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what actually happened">
    Tell us what you observed: an error message, unexpected behavior, a missing button, etc. If you see an error message or code, copy and paste the exact text — don't paraphrase it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include your workspace and account details">
    Provide your **workspace name** (found in **Settings → General**) and the **email address** on your Gugu account. We cannot look up your account without at least one of these.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List the steps to reproduce the issue">
    Walk us through exactly what you did, step by step. For example:
    1. Opened the dashboard
    2. Clicked **New Content**
    3. Filled in the title and body
    4. Clicked **Save** — received a 500 error

    Reproducible issues get fixed much faster than vague reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach screenshots or recordings">
    A screenshot of the error or a short screen recording is worth a thousand words. Attach them directly to your email or paste them into your forum post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include browser and OS information">
    Let us know which **browser** (and version) and **operating system** you're using. For API issues, include the request method, endpoint URL, and a sanitized version of the request (remove your API key before sharing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  For billing inquiries — such as refund requests, invoice questions, or plan changes — email **billing@gugu.app** directly. This ensures your message reaches the right team without delay.
</Info>
