Most issues in Gugu have a straightforward fix. This page walks you through the most common problems you might encounter and explains exactly how to resolve them. Work through the steps in order — they're arranged from the simplest checks to more involved solutions.

<Accordion title="I can't log in">
  If you're unable to sign in to your Gugu account, try the following steps in order.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Double-check your credentials">
      Confirm you're using the email address registered with Gugu and that Caps Lock is off. Passwords are case-sensitive.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Reset your password">
      On the login page, click **Forgot password?** and follow the instructions sent to your email. Reset links expire after 60 minutes.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check for an account lock">
      After several failed attempts, Gugu temporarily locks your account for security. Wait **10 minutes** and try again, or use the password reset flow to unlock it immediately.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Try a different browser or incognito mode">
      Browser extensions (especially ad blockers and password managers) can interfere with the login form. Open an incognito/private window and attempt to sign in again.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Clear cookies and cache">
      Stale session cookies sometimes prevent authentication. Clear your browser's cookies and cached data for `gugu.app`, then reload and try again.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>
    If you sign in with Google or GitHub, make sure you're selecting the correct provider on the login page. Attempting to log in with email/password when your account uses social login will always fail.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Search isn't returning results">
  If the search bar returns no results or results that seem outdated, the index may need to catch up or your query may need adjustment.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your search syntax">
      Gugu's search supports plain keywords, quoted phrases (`"exact phrase"`), and field filters (e.g., `type:article`). Avoid special characters unless you intend them as filters.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Wait for indexing to complete">
      Newly created or updated content can take up to **2 minutes** to appear in search results. If you just saved a document, wait a moment and search again.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify content isn't archived">
      Archived items are excluded from default searches. Use the **All content** filter in the search panel to include them.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Re-index your workspace">
      Go to **Settings → Search** and click **Re-index workspace**. This rebuilds the index from scratch and typically completes within a few minutes for most workspaces.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Info>
    Search indexes are updated in real time for Pro and Enterprise plans. Free plan workspaces use a batch index that refreshes every 5 minutes.
  </Info>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My content isn't saving">
  If changes to your content aren't being persisted, one of the following is usually the cause.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Look for the save indicator">
      The editor shows a **Saving…** spinner in the toolbar while changes are being written. If it's stuck, check your internet connection.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check your connection">
      Open another tab and confirm you have internet access. Gugu requires a stable connection to auto-save. The editor will retry when connectivity is restored.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm you have edit permissions">
      Viewers cannot save changes. Ask a workspace Admin to upgrade your role to **Editor** or **Admin** in **Settings → Team**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Try a hard refresh">
      Press `Ctrl + Shift + R` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd + Shift + R` (macOS) to force-reload the page. If unsaved changes are present, Gugu will warn you before reloading.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check workspace storage">
      If your workspace has reached its storage limit, new saves are blocked. Go to **Settings → Billing** to review usage and upgrade if needed.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Warning>
    If the editor displays a red **Conflict** banner, another team member saved changes at the same time. Use the conflict resolution panel to choose which version to keep before saving again.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I'm not receiving email notifications">
  Gugu sends notifications for comments, mentions, sharing events, and system alerts. If you're not receiving them, work through these checks.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your spam folder">
      Emails from `notifications@gugu.app` occasionally land in spam. Mark them as **Not spam** to train your mail provider.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Review your notification preferences">
      Go to **Settings → Notifications** and confirm that the notification types you expect are enabled. Each category (comments, mentions, billing) can be toggled individually.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify your email address is confirmed">
      Notifications are only sent to verified email addresses. Go to **Settings → Profile** and check whether your email has a **Verified** badge. If not, click **Resend verification**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check with your IT or email admin">
      Corporate email systems sometimes block bulk senders. Ask your IT team to allowlist `@gugu.app` and the IP ranges listed in our [email delivery docs](https://docs.gugu.app/email-delivery).
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The app is slow or unresponsive">
  Performance issues are usually caused by browser state, network conditions, or a temporary spike in load. Here's how to narrow it down.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check the status page">
      Visit [status.gugu.app](https://status.gugu.app) to see if there's a known incident or degraded performance affecting all users.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Test in another browser">
      Try opening Gugu in a different browser or in an incognito window. If performance improves, the issue is likely caused by a browser extension or heavy cache.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Clear your browser cache">
      A bloated cache can slow down asset loading significantly. Clear cached data for `gugu.app` and reload.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Reduce open tabs and memory usage">
      Gugu's editor is memory-intensive. Close unused browser tabs, especially ones running other rich web apps.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check your network speed">
      Run a speed test at [fast.com](https://fast.com). Gugu works best with a connection of **10 Mbps or faster**. On slower connections, disable auto-saving of large media files in **Settings → Editor**.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Tip>
    If you're on a large workspace with thousands of content items, try using the **Filter** sidebar to narrow your dashboard view — rendering large content lists can be demanding on older hardware.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="API requests are returning 401 errors">
  A `401 Unauthorized` response means Gugu could not authenticate your request. The response body looks like this:

  ```json
  {
    "error": {
      "code": "unauthorized",
      "message": "Invalid or expired API key"
    }
  }
  ```

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Confirm the Authorization header format">
      Every request must include a valid `Authorization` header using the Bearer scheme:

      ```http
      Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY
      ```

      A missing header, wrong scheme (e.g., `Basic`), or extra whitespace will all produce a 401.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check that the key hasn't expired">
      Go to **Settings → API Keys** and verify the key's expiry date. If it has expired, revoke it and generate a new one.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm the key belongs to the correct workspace">
      API keys are scoped to a single workspace. If you're calling the API for workspace B with a key from workspace A, you'll receive a 401.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Regenerate the key if in doubt">
      If you can't identify the cause, revoke the existing key, generate a fresh one, update your environment variables or secrets store, and retry.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="API requests are returning 429 errors (rate limited)">
  A `429 Too Many Requests` response means your API key has exceeded its allowed request rate. The response body looks like this:

  ```json
  {
    "error": {
      "code": "rate_limited",
      "message": "You have exceeded the rate limit. Please retry after 30 seconds."
    }
  }
  ```

  The response also includes a `Retry-After` header indicating how many seconds to wait before retrying.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Respect the Retry-After header">
      Read the `Retry-After` value from the response header and pause your client for at least that many seconds before resending the request.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Implement exponential backoff">
      For automated clients, use exponential backoff with jitter rather than a fixed delay. This prevents all retries from hitting the API at exactly the same moment.

      ```python
      import time, random

      def backoff(attempt: int) -> float:
          return min(2 ** attempt + random.uniform(0, 1), 60)
      ```
    </Step>
    <Step title="Batch or cache requests">
      Review your integration for opportunities to batch multiple operations into a single request, or cache responses for data that doesn't change frequently.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Upgrade your plan">
      If your use case genuinely requires higher throughput, upgrade to the Pro or Enterprise plan for significantly higher rate limits. See the [FAQ](/help/faq#what-are-the-rate-limits-for-the-api) for the full limits table.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't invite a collaborator">
  If the **Invite Member** flow fails or the invitation never arrives, one of the following is usually the cause.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your seat limit">
      Each plan includes a maximum number of workspace seats. Go to **Settings → Billing** to see how many seats you've used. If you're at the limit, upgrade your plan or remove an inactive member first.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm you have Admin permissions">
      Only users with the **Admin** role can send invitations. If you don't see the **Invite Member** button, ask an existing Admin to send the invite or to promote your role.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check for a typo in the email address">
      Re-enter the invitee's email carefully. If the email domain uses a strict spam policy, the invitation may be silently dropped on their end.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Resend or revoke and re-invite">
      Go to **Settings → Team → Pending Invitations**, revoke the existing invite, and send a fresh one. Invitations expire after 7 days.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Ask the invitee to check spam">
      Invitation emails from `invites@gugu.app` can end up in spam folders, especially for corporate addresses.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Info>
    Inviting someone who already has a personal Gugu account will link their existing account to your workspace — they won't need to create a new one.
  </Info>
</Accordion>

<Note>

</Note>
